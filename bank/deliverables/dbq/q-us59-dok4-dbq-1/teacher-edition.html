The question

Using all three documents and your knowledge of Cold War origins (US.59–US.60), construct an argument that evaluates the relative roles of ideology and geopolitical strategy in causing the Cold War. Apply HIPP analysis to at least two of the three documents and use outside historical knowledge to support your argument. Your response

  1. what each document reveals about U.S./Western perceptions of the Soviet threat
  2. whether the Cold War was primarily an ideological conflict or a geopolitical competition for power; and
  3. whether alternative outcomes were possible given the structural conditions of 1945–1947

Scoring guide

Pts6-point scale
6Exemplary. Presents a precise, historically arguable thesis on the ideology/geopolitics question. Applies full HIPP analysis to at least two documents, identifying historical context, audience, purpose, AND point of view for each. Uses outside historical evidence (minimum 3 specific facts) not from the documents. Addresses all three prompt components: perceptions, causation debate, and alternative outcomes. Demonstrates sophisticated understanding of historiographical debate.
5Advanced. Clear thesis. Full HIPP applied to two documents (may be incomplete on one element for one document). Two pieces of outside evidence. Addresses two of three prompt components thoroughly.
4Proficient. Arguable thesis. HIPP partially applied to two documents (addresses 2–3 elements per document). One piece of outside evidence. Addresses at least two prompt components.
3Adequate. General thesis. Analyzes at least two documents but without systematic HIPP. Uses document evidence but limited outside knowledge. Addresses one prompt component clearly.
2Developing. Basic claim. References at least one document but analysis is mostly summary. Minimal outside evidence. Prompt components addressed superficially.
1Beginning. Attempts a response. May reference documents but without analysis. Little to no outside evidence. Does not address prompt components.
0No response or off-topic.
Expected evidence and document analysis. DOCUMENT ANALYSIS EXPECTATIONS: For Document A — HIPP should identify Kennan's diplomatic vantage point, State Dept. audience, warning purpose, and his trained diplomat's point of view that shaped his perception. For Document B — HIPP should note Churchill's political status, the American audience (Truman present), the galvanizing purpose, and Churchill's imperial Great Power point of view. For Document C — HIPP should identify the immediate Greece/Turkey crisis, the congressional audience, the funding request purpose, and Truman's Manichean framing as a political strategy. KEY EVIDENCE EXPECTED: Yalta and Potsdam tensions; Soviet satellite states (1945–1948); Soviet rejection of Marshall Plan; Berlin Blockade (1948–1949); Soviet atomic test (1949); Chinese Communist victory (1949); NSC-68 (1950) as ideological escalation.
Sourcing. Every document above carries its own citation as written in the item record. Verify each against the holding institution before classroom use — an unsourced primary source is worse than a missing one.
