The sources

Document A

National Industrial Recovery Act, Section 1 (Declaration of Policy), June 16, 1933 (National Archives, public domain)

A national emergency productive of widespread unemployment and disorganization of industry, which burdens interstate and foreign commerce...is hereby declared to exist. It is hereby declared to be the policy of Congress to remove obstructions to the free flow of interstate and foreign commerce which tend to diminish the amount thereof; and to provide for the general welfare by promoting the organization of industry for the purpose of cooperative action among trade groups...

The question

PROMPT: Using all three documents and your knowledge of the New Deal (1933–1938), construct an argument that answers: How did the New Deal evolve from the First New Deal (1933–1934) to the Second New Deal (1935–1938), and what does this evolution reveal about FDR's response to political pressure from critics on both the left and right? In your response, you MUST: 1. Analyze each document using the HIPP framework — at least ONE HIPP element per document. 2. Place each document within the First or Second New Deal phase and explain what governing philosophy it reflects. 3. Construct a central argument using all three documents AND at least two outside historical facts explaining how the shift from Document A to Documents B and C reflects a meaningful philosophical and political evolution in the New Deal. 4. Evaluate: Was the Second New Deal a genuine ideological shift toward redistribution and worker empowerment, or primarily a political strategy to neutralize critics like Huey Long and the Supreme Court?
