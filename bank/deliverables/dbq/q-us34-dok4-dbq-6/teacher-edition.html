The question

Using BOTH documents and your knowledge of mass media and consumer culture (US.34) and economic prosperity and speculation (US.36), write a multi-paragraph response that applies HIPP to each document and constructs an argument about how the consumer culture promoted in the 1920s created the conditions for economic collapse. Your response must analyze both documents' Historical context, Intended audience, Purpose, and Point of view; explain what each document's perspective reveals about its author's knowledge and interests; and use outside evidence to support your argument.

Scoring guide

Pts6-point scale
6Exemplary. Full HIPP for both documents including Coolidge's limitation (he cannot foresee the crash) and Senate Committee's retrospective advantage. Precise argument linking consumer culture ideology (Doc A) to structural vulnerability (Doc B). At least two pieces of outside evidence. Cross-era connection to Great Depression.
5Advanced. HIPP (3+ elements) for both. Clear argument. Both documents cited. At least one piece of outside evidence.
4Proficient. HIPP (2+ elements) for both. Argument present. Both documents used.
3Adequate. Some HIPP for both. Argument present but underdeveloped.
2Developing. HIPP for only one document. Weak argument.
1Beginning. Summary without HIPP. No clear argument.
0No response or completely off-topic.
Expected evidence and document analysis. DOCUMENT ANALYSIS EXPECTATIONS: Coolidge's 'limitation' (no foreknowledge of crash); Senate Committee's retrospective position; both documents produced in very different political contexts. KEY EVIDENCE EXPECTED: Installment buying, radio/advertising, margin buying, Black Tuesday, Mellon tax policy, Smoot-Hawley, Hoover's response, connection to US.39 Great Depression.
