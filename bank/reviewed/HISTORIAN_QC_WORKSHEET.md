# Historian QC worksheet

**Nothing here is verified and nothing here is approved.** Every line is a claim to confirm, generated from the item text. This tool has no access to a library; it cannot tell you whether a date is right, only that the date is a thing that is either right or wrong. The verdict is yours.

Claims are ordered worst-first. A **negative-existence** claim leads because it is the cheapest sentence to write and the costliest to check — `PSTIM-0041`'s misconception label asserted the Berlin Blockade was never ended by negotiation, which is false, and no gate read that field.

## Look at these first

*This section is kept SHORT on purpose. An `outside-declared-era` check sat here until its first run flagged five items and all five were correct history — a standard's era is where TDOE places it in the course, not a boundary on its content. A heading that is wrong five times out of five teaches you to skip it, so year spans moved to orientation and only claims that genuinely need a verdict appear here.*
